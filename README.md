# Black Hole Visualiser

**Drag a black hole around and watch it bend light.**
Open `index.html` in a browser.

## Controls
- **Drag** the black hole
- Change the **mass** to change its pull
- Toggle **DISK** (glowing disk around it) and **LENS** (light bending)

## How it works
Gravity pulls on light as well as matter. Near the black hole the image is distorted (**gravitational lensing**), and a bright disk of hot material spins around it.

## Limits (honest)
It is a **visual model, not a physics-exact simulation**. Real black holes need general relativity to calculate precisely.

## What I learned
Light can be bent by gravity, and bigger mass means stronger bending.
